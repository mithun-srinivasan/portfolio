Hi! Welcome to my blog.

I built this space as part of my personal portfolio. The entire site is deliberately built without frameworks, bundlers, or package managers: plain HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript, styled in a restrained red, white, and black palette with self-hosted JetBrains Mono.

Here I will be writing about:

- Linux configuration, window managers, and personal tooling.
- Open source contributions and building FOSS tools like `ditch-windows`.
- Computer science coursework at MSEC and data science at IIT Madras.
- What breaks when learning, experimenting, and hacking on developer tools.

Thanks for stopping by. Feel free to explore [selected work](/#work) or drop a message to say hello.
